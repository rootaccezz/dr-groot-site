Inside the Dr. Groot Story (About the Brand)

LG Household & Health Care was founded in 1947 and launched its first shampoo in 1956. Since then, the company has focused on research and innovation and has built a strong position in the Korean FMCG market.

Dr. Groot was introduced in 2017 after years of research at LG H&H’s Genetic Analysis Lab. Scientists studied thousands of genetic data points to better understand hair loss and scalp health. This research helped identify important factors related to hair strength and scalp condition.

The word “Dr.” represents scientific research, and “Groot” stands for grow root, which reflects its focus on scalp and root health. Dr Groot was developed by dermatologists, pharmacists, and researchers through a detailed study of the scalp. The products are clinically tested to promote voluminous and healthier-looking hair within just two weeks.

The products are formulated to cure a range of scalp issues, which include dryness, breakage, sebum production, skin sensitivity, and thinning of hair. The premium ingredients such as biotin, prebiotics, rosemary, and niacinamide promote healthier-looking hair and reduce hair fall.

  • Over 60,000 genetic data points analysed by 138 dedicated researchers
  • Supported by 528 registered patents and 148 clinical studies
