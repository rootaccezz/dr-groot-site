Your 30-Day Dr. Groot Hair Care Plan [Full Guide]

Hair loss affects more people than most realize. Studies suggest that up to 85% of men and approximately 33-40% of women experience some level of hair thinning or hair loss during their lifetime. Most people are treating the symptoms of thin, brittle, shedding hair and ignoring the cause: an unhealthy scalp. Dr. Groot’s Scalp Revitalizing Solution line is a clinically tested, biotin and caffeine-powered system built on one belief: healthy hair starts at the scalp.

In this guide, we will explain a complete 30-day routine for using Dr. Groot products and what to expect after following the proper process.

Why Scalp Health Comes First

Most people condition their hair ends, protect the length, and forget about the foundation. But hair grows from follicles embedded in your scalp. If that environment is clogged with buildup, low on moisture, or chronically inflamed, no serum or treatment will move the needle.

Dr. Groot’s entire philosophy is built on this principle. Every product in the Scalp Revitalizing Solution range is designed to optimize the scalp environment first, so hair can grow thicker, stronger, and healthier on its own.

What the Clinical Data Actually Says

Before you commit 30 days, it’s worth knowing what the brand has tested and what the evidence shows:

  • In a two-week clinical study of 24 subjects using the full system daily, participants saw an 82.2% reduction in hair loss due to breakage
  • A separate study of 22 subjects using the shampoo and Miracle in Shower Treatment together showed a 169% improvement in scalp moisture
  • The same study found 76% removal of buildup that can clog scalp pores
  • 100% of subjects showed measurable improvement in visible scalp redness from dry skin.

This isn’t a marketing language. These are dermatologist-tested, quantified outcomes, which is why the 30-day plan is structured to mirror the regimen used in the trials.

30-Day Dr. Groot Hair Care Routine

Week 1 to 2: Build the Base (Daily Shampoo + Treatment)

Goal: Clear scalp buildup, restore moisture balance, and prep follicles for growth.

Week 1 expectations: Your scalp will likely feel cleaner and lighter after the first wash. Some people notice reduced itchiness or oiliness by days 3 to 5.

Week 2 expectations: Scalp moisture levels start stabilizing. This is when the prebiotic complex in the shampoo begins supporting follicle nourishment. Hair may feel noticeably softer and more manageable.

Every wash day routine (aim for 3 to 4 times per week):

  1. Wet your hair and scalp thoroughly with warm water.
  2. Apply the Hair Thickening Shampoo to your scalp, not just the lengths. Massage gently in circular motions. This is not just cleaning; the massage itself helps stimulate blood flow to the follicles.
  3. Lather, then rinse thoroughly.
  4. Follow immediately with the Miracle In Shower Treatment. Apply it to the scalp and mid-lengths, leave it on for 2 to 3 minutes, then rinse.
  5. Pat your hair dry and avoid rubbing it.

Week 3 to 4: Activate with the Serum

Goal: Reinforce progress, target thinning areas, and stimulate the follicles at the root level.

Week 3 expectations: Hair loss during washing may reduce noticeably. The serum’s lightweight formula should not weigh hair down, and most hair types report little to no greasiness.

Week 4 expectations: This is typically when visible thickening starts becoming more noticeable. Hair density at the roots may look improved, and your overall hair texture can feel stronger and more resilient.

After Every Wash (On Towel-Dried Hair)

  1. Complete your shampoo and Miracle In Shower Treatment steps as outlined above.
  2. Section your hair to expose the scalp, especially around the crown, hairline, or any areas where you notice thinning.
  3. Roll the Dr. Groot Hair Thickening Roll-On Serum directly onto the scalp using the metal tip. Apply it in rows across each section.
  4. Gently massage the serum into the scalp with your fingertips, and do not rinse it out.

The niacinamide in the serum helps improve scalp circulation and supports a stronger skin barrier. Studies suggest that caffeine may help keep hair in the active growth phase for a longer time, which means the follicles spend more time growing hair and less time resting. The menthol gives a cooling feeling that helps show the product is absorbing into the scalp where it is needed.

Source: NCBI

Who This Routine Is Best For

The system is specifically designed for all scalp types, with extra effectiveness for:

  • Thin or fine hair looking for volume and density
  • Hair experiencing breakage or post-stress shedding
  • Dry or sensitive scalps prone to redness or flakiness
  • Anyone transitioning off harsh, silicone-heavy products

Because the formula is free from parabens, sulfates, and silicones, it’s safe for color-treated hair. The prebiotics and biotin work for all hair textures.

Daily Habits That Make the Routine Work Better

The Dr. Groot system does the heavy lifting, but a few daily habits help keep the results compounding:

  • Sleep on silk or satin: Cotton pillowcases create friction that causes breakage overnight, exactly what you have been working against.
  • Lower your heat tool temperature: During the 30 days, try keeping hot tools under 180°C (356°F). Less thermal stress means less breakage, which helps keep your reduced-shedding numbers low.
  • Do not skip scalp massage: Even on non-wash days, spend 3 to 5 minutes massaging your scalp with a silicone massage brush. Research suggests this can improve hair thickness over time by stimulating dermal papilla cells.
  • Track what you are losing: Clean your brush before day 1 and after day 30. Visual evidence of reduced shedding is one of the most motivating parts of the process.

Final Thought

Thirty days is a realistic commitment, and according to Dr. Groot’s own clinical data, you may not even need all 30 days to start seeing results. Two weeks of consistent use with the full system produced measurable results across all studied subjects.

The key is consistency and using the products together. The shampoo, Miracle in Shower Treatment, and Roll-On Serum aren’t designed to work in isolation. They build on each other, and that’s what makes the difference between a hair care routine and an actual hair care system.

FAQs

Can I use Dr. Groot if I have color-treated hair?
↑
Yes. The Scalp Revitalizing Solution line is formulated without parabens, sulfates, or silicones. These three ingredients are most likely to strip color or damage chemically treated hair. The formula is also dermatologically tested and suitable for all scalp types, including sensitive ones.
Is this routine suitable for both men and women?
↑
Yes. Hair fall and thinning affect both men and women, and the ingredients in the Dr. Groot system (biotin, caffeine, rosemary oil, niacinamide) address the shared root causes: scalp buildup, poor follicle circulation, and weakened hair structure.
Do I need to use all three products for the plan to work?
↑
The full system Shampoo, Miracle In Shower Treatment, and Roll-On Serum were used in Dr. Groot’s clinical trials. The results, including the 82.2% reduction in breakage, are based on the combined regimen used regularly.
