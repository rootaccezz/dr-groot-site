Where to Buy Dr. Groot Products: Complete Buying Guide

Dr. Groot has quickly become one of the most talked-about K-beauty hair care brands in the U.S., but knowing where to buy authentic products at the best prices isn’t always straightforward. You’ve heard about Dr. Groot, maybe spotted it on your Amazon feed or seen it recommended in a Reddit thread, but you’re still not sure where to actually buy it.

If you’re looking to buy the Dr. Groot Haircare Line and want to get it right the first time, this guide covers every trusted source, current pricing, and the buying tips that can make a real difference.

Where to Buy Dr. Groot Products Online

Not all hair care purchases are equal. Buy from the wrong seller, and you risk counterfeit products, inflated prices, or a product range so overwhelming that you don’t know where to start. Let’s look at the options where you can get original products at a very reasonable price range.

1. Dr. Groot Website (dr-groot.net)

To explore Dr. Groot shampoo and scalp care products, dr-groot.net offers detailed information on formula selections, scalp compatibility, and product features to help inform buying decisions.

For those comparing different Dr. Groot options, this resource helps clarify the benefits of each formula along with suitability for specific hair and scalp needs.

2. LG Beauty

The official LG Beauty store is the brand’s home base in the U.S. market. Shopping here means you’re buying directly from the manufacturer. There’s no fear of third-party sellers or authenticity concerns. It’s also where new launches and exclusive bundles tend to appear first.

3. Amazon

Amazon is the most accessible option for most shoppers worldwide, with fast shipping and straightforward returns. The Hair Thickening Shampoo is listed at 33.00 one-time, with a Subscribe & Save discount bringing it down to *29.70. The Scalp Soothing Shampoo and Purifying Clay Shampoo are also priced at $33.00 one-time. The platform usually offers Subscribe & Save options that further reduce the price.

More Buying Options

Sephora

For prestige beauty shoppers, Dr. Groot debuted online at Sephora.com on April 1, 2026, followed by in-store availability at select doors starting May 15. From August 2026, distribution is expanding into approximately 400 Sephora doors across the United States, reaching the majority of Sephora locations nationwide by year end. Sephora stocks hero products from the Scalp Revitalizing Solution line, including the Hair Thickening Shampoo and the five-second Miracle In-Shower Treatment.

Costco

If you want to save money, check Costco. Since October 2025, Dr. Groot has expanded into more than 600 Costco warehouses across the United States, Canada, and Mexico. They sell large 23.6 oz twin packs of the Hair Thickening Shampoo and Conditioner. Buying these value-size packs from Costco gives you a lower price per ounce compared to standard retail bottles, making it a practical option for regular users.

Olive Young Global

Living outside the USA? LG Beauty only ships within the United States, but you still have another option. Olive Young Global is a trusted Korean store that ships to more than 150 countries. They sell many Dr. Groot products and offer fast shipping directly from South Korea.

Quick Buying Tips

Match the product to your scalp, not your hair

The Scalp Revitalizing Solution range is designed for condition-specific use, including a thickening range for thin or damaged hair, a soothing range for dry scalps, and a purifying clay range for oily scalps. Picking your product based on your scalp type rather than your hair type can help you achieve faster results.

Buy it as a regimen, not just a shampoo

Clinical results showing an 82.2% reduction in breakage and a 169% improvement in scalp moisture are based on using the Scalp Revitalizing Solution products together. A standalone shampoo works, but the full routine delivers the complete results.

Use Subscribe & Save on Amazon if you plan to use it consistently

The first Subscribe & Save order can save you up to 10 to 15% on top of the already discounted price, making it useful for a product you’ll be using daily.

Check for bundles on LG Beauty

The official store periodically offers shampoo and treatment sets that provide better per-unit value than buying individual products separately.

Final Thoughts

Finding Dr. Groot products, including shampoos, isn’t the hard part. Finding the right place to buy them and choosing the right product for your scalp is what really matters. It doesn’t matter where you shop, whether through the Dr. Groot website for personalized guidance, LG Beauty for direct brand authenticity, or Amazon for convenience and Subscribe & Save savings. What matters most is that you’re investing in a formula built on real science and clinically verified results.

FAQs

Is Dr. Groot available in physical stores or only online?
↑
Dr. Groot products are stocked in physical retail stores as well as online storefronts. Physical distribution includes over 600 Costco warehouses across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, alongside a nationwide rollout across approximately 400 Sephora doors through late 2026 following its Sephora.com debut. For online purchases, the complete product collection, current promotional pricing, and direct shipping remain readily available via LG Beauty, Amazon, and Olive Young Global for international delivery.
Is it cheaper to buy Dr. Groot on Amazon or LG Beauty?
↑
On Amazon, the regular price is 33.00* per product, with Subscribe & Save bringing it down to *29.70 or lower, depending on your subscription tier. LG Beauty often offers unique bundles, loyalty points, or promotional pricing (such as 15% off hero items). Always compare the two platforms before you place your order to find the best deal.
How do I know I’m buying an authentic Dr. Groot product?
↑
Stick to the three verified sources: LG Beauty, Dr. Groot website, and the official Dr. Groot storefront on Amazon. On Amazon, ensure the product is sold directly by “Dr. Groot” or “LG Household & Health Care.” Always check the seller’s name before adding to your cart to avoid third-party resellers and ensure you receive a genuine product.
