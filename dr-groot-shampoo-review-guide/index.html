Dr. Groot Shampoo Review: Ingredients, Clinical Results & Real Customer Reviews

Whenever you search for a Korean hair care brand, you’ve probably come across recommendations for Dr Groot Shampoo. But is it actually worth it? Dr. Groot doesn’t just make one shampoo and call it a day. The brand’s Scalp Revitalizing Solution line includes three distinct shampoos, each designed for a different scalp condition.

Here’s a detailed breakdown of all three shampoos, what they contain, what the clinical data actually shows, who each one is made for, and what actual customers are saying about them.

Dr Groot Hair Thickening Shampoo

  • Best for: Thin, damaged, or fine hair with hair loss due to breakage
  • Price: $33.00 (13 fl oz)
  • Award Winner and Best Seller on Amazon and LG Beauty
Buy on Amazon

The Dr. Groot Shampoo is part of the Scalp Revitalizing Solution range. It is a clinically developed line built on the principle that hair care starts at the scalp. The shampoo is designed to deeply cleanse the scalp, remove follicle-clogging buildup, and create the right environment for thicker, stronger hair growth.

Key Ingredients

The Hair Thickening Shampoo is formulated with four core active ingredients:

  • Biotin is an essential vitamin that helps support keratin for healthy-looking hair.
  • Capsulated Prebiotics are added to help support nourished hair follicles for stronger, healthier-looking hair.
  • Rosemary Oil invigorates the hair and scalp for healthier, thicker, stronger hair, and
  • Caffeine helps deliver more nutrients to hair follicles.

The full ingredient list also includes brewer’s yeast, Bifida Ferment Lysate, Lactobacillus Ferment Lysate, Centella Asiatica Leaf Extract, Hyaluronic Acid, Salicylic Acid, Niacinamide, and Panax Ginseng Root Extract, which make this more than a basic shampoo formula.

The formula contains no parabens, silicones, or sulfates, is dermatologically tested, and is safe for color-treated hair.

Clinically Proven Results

Based on a two-week clinical test of subjects using the full Scalp Revitalizing Solution regimen:

  • 82.2% reduction in hair loss due to breakage
  • 169% improvement in scalp hydration (moisture)
  • 76% reduction in buildup clogging scalp pores
  • 100% of subjects showed measurable improvement in visible scalp redness from dry skin.

Who It’s For

Hair Thickening Shampoo is suitable for anyone with thinning hair, breakage-prone hair, or a scalp that needs both deep cleansing and active nourishment. It is safe for color-treated hair, dermatologically tested, and free of parabens, silicones, and sulfates.

Dr. Groot Purifying Clay Shampoo

  • Best for: Oily scalp, excess sebum, buildup, and scalp odor
  • Price: $33.00 (one-time) | $29.70 (subscription)
Buy on LG Beauty

The Purifying Clay Shampoo is built around three types of clay; it draws out excess oil, micro-dust, and deep-seated buildup from the scalp in a single wash. It maintains moisture at the ends to prevent the “dry-roots-versus-parched-ends” problem that oily scalp sufferers know too well.

Key Ingredients

  • Three Kinds of Fine Clay (white clay, herbal clay, and glacial clay) work together to absorb excess oil and draw out impurities from the scalp. Each clay type has different mineral properties, making it more effective than a single-clay formula at balancing oiliness without over-drying.
  • Biotin is carried through even in this oil-focused formula, as follicle support continues regardless of scalp type.
  • Salicylic Acid helps to cleanse buildup and clogged pores on the scalp.

Clinically Results

Based on clinical testing conducted on 21 to 32 subjects:

  • 92% scalp purification after just one use (tested on subjects with micro-dust on scalps)
  • 48% reduction in excess sebum after two weeks
  • 100% of subjects showed measurably improved scalp hydration after two weeks
  • 100% of subjects showed measurably improved scalp pore elasticity after two weeks
  • 100% of subjects showed measurable improvement in scalp pore odor within 24 hours after two weeks of use.

Who It’s For

The shampoo is for anyone with an oily or congested scalp, frequent buildup, or greasy hair at the roots within a day or two of washing, or scalp odor concerns. It is also useful as a periodic clarifying treatment for any scalp type that needs a deeper reset.

Dr. Groot Professional Bonding System Bond Fortifying Shampoo

  • Best for: Dry, damaged, fragile hair after heat styling, color, or chemical treatments
  • Price: $30.00 (one-time) | $27.00 (subscription)
Buy on LG Beauty

Most shampoos in Dr. Groot’s range are built around scalp health. The Bond Fortifying Shampoo takes a different approach; it targets the hair strand itself, specifically the internal bond structure that gets broken down by heat, color, bleach, and chemical processes. It is the first step in Dr. Groot’s Professional Bonding System, a four-product repair regimen that works progressively through the wash routine to rebuild damaged hair from the inside out.

Key Ingredients

  • Keratin is the most important ingredient in this formula. Keratin begins the repair process from the very first wash, helping hair look visibly smoother and feel stronger immediately by strengthening hair bonds.
  • Silk Protein is infused as a detangling agent that works alongside keratin to help lock in moisture and visibly improve elasticity so hair looks and feels softer.

Clinically Results

  • Based on a clinical test of 22 subjects using the full system (Shampoo, Keratin Water Treatment, Conditioner, and Serum together).
  • Clinically proven to repair and measurably thicken the look of hair in just one use.
  • Restores smoothness and softness.
  • Suitable for all hair types and textures.
  • Delicate scent of white floral musk.
  • Hair damage improved by 1.5 times compared to untreated hair, based on a clinical test conducted on 55 hair tresses.
  • The one-use visible improvement result is notable, meaning the formula does not require weeks of buildup to produce a noticeable change in how hair looks and feels.

Suitable For

Anyone whose hair has been through repeated heat styling, bleaching, coloring, perming, or other chemical processes. Also ideal for hair that is dry, brittle, or prone to breakage, regardless of chemical history.

Dr. Groot Shampoo vs. Regular Shampoo: Quick Comparison

Most regular shampoos are designed to cleanse the hair strands. They help remove oil, product buildup, and dirt. Dr. Groot operates on a different premise entirely.

The brand positions scalp care as an extension of skincare. The formula is designed to cleanse and moisturize the scalp while also providing benefits to the hair. It especially features salicylic acid to unclog pores, arginine to soothe the skin, and panthenol for nourishment.

Where a standard shampoo stops at the strand, Dr. Groot addresses the scalp environment, which is often the root cause of thinning, oiliness, dryness, and breakage. It won’t grow back hair that has already been lost.
