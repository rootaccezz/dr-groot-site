FAQs

Is Dr. Groot suitable for all hair types?
↑
Yes, the entire Scalp Revitalizing Solution range is formulated for all hair types and textures. It is suitable for curly, thick, and color-treated hair. The products are free of parabens and silicone, which makes them gentle for regular use.
Can I use Tonic with other scalp treatments?
↑
Yes. The Scalp Relief Tonic is a no-rinse, lightweight spray that layers well with other products. Apply it before heavier treatments like serums or oils so it absorbs directly into the scalp. It can be layered with other scalp treatments, but avoid mixing it with heavy treatments.
How does niacinamide benefit the scalp?
↑
Niacinamide strengthens the scalp’s natural barrier, regulates sebum production, and reduces inflammation. In Dr. Groot’s formulas, it also visibly reduces scalp redness and dryness, which can contribute to excess shedding when left untreated
