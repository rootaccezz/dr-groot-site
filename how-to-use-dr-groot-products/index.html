Just like your skin needs a cleanser, moisturizer, and targeted treatments, the same logic applies to your hair and scalp. They require proper care both during and after washing.   3. Hair Thickening Conditioner reduces hair loss from breakage, provides volume, and softens hair.
  4. Hair Thickening Roll-On Serum offers precise spot care for thinning areas.

How to Use Dr. Groot Scalp Revitalizing Solution [Step-by-Step]

The order to use Dr. Groot Scalp Revitalizing Solution is very important. Skipping or swapping will reduce effects. Here’s how to use it correctly:

Step 1: Hair Thickening Shampoo

Start with completely wet hair. Apply the shampoo to your hair and scalp, then gently massage to form a lather. Rinse thoroughly with water. The shampoo is formulated for all hair types and is safe for color-treated hair.

Step 2: Miracle In-Shower Treatment

After shampooing, apply the treatment to wet hair and scalp, then gently massage it in. Rinse thoroughly with water. The liquid turns creamy on contact for deep nourishment.

Step 3: Hair Thickening Conditioner

Directly after rinsing the treatment, apply the appropriate amount of conditioner. Apply a small amount to your hair and scalp, massage it gently, and rinse with water.

Step 4: Hair Thickening Roll-On Serum

Click the serum button to dispense a pea-sized amount, then use the roller ball to massage it into troubled areas of the scalp in circular motions. Repeat if needed if hair is dry. The smooth metal roller helps apply the serum evenly to the thinning areas of the scalp.

Clinically Proven Results and Benefits

The Scalp Revitalizing Hair Thickening Shampoo helps clean away buildup from the scalp and keeps it in better condition for stronger-looking hair. The Hair Thickening Conditioner helps reduce hair loss caused by breakage and makes hair feel softer, fuller, and easier to manage. The Miracle In-Shower Treatment gently exfoliates the scalp, adds moisture, and smooths the hair from root to tip, helping create a healthier base for your hair.

The Hair Thickening Roll-On Serum is made for targeted use on thinning areas. It helps reduce breakage and keeps the scalp nourished. The metal tip makes it easy to apply exactly where needed, while the menthol gives a light cooling feeling. Its lightweight formula absorbs quickly and doesn’t leave the hair feeling heavy.

Final Thoughts

Healthy hair starts at the scalp, and Dr. Groot Hair Revitalizing Solution cares for your scalp and hair growth. If you follow the steps in sequence without swapping, the results will speak for themselves. The full set is clinically proven to reduce hair loss due to breakage by 82.2% in just two weeks.

FAQs

Where to buy Dr Groot Scalp Revitalizing Solution?
↑
You can purchase the complete set directly from Dr. Groot website, LG Beauty, or from Amazon. All options include the full 4-piece Dr Groot Scalp Revitalizing Solution set for a healthier-looking scalp and hair.
Can I use Dr Groot Scalp Revitalizing Solution on wet hair?
↑
Yes, the in-shower steps are designed specifically for wet hair. After shampooing, apply the Miracle In-Shower Treatment to wet hair and scalp, massage gently, then rinse thoroughly. The roll-on serum is the only step applied post-shower on damp or dry hair.
What is the texture of the serum, and how does it work?
↑
The serum features a menthol-infused watery gel texture that is lightweight and non-sticky, with a roll-on metal tip designed for precise spot care on the scalp. Simply click to dispense, roll onto troubled areas, and massage in circular motions.
Do I need to use all 4 products together for better results?
↑
You can use individual products, but the clinical results are based on the full set used together. The complete 4-piece set is clinically proven to reduce hair loss due to breakage by 82.2% in just two weeks when used as a consistent routine.
